## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They help the site work correctly and, with your consent, help us understand how visitors use the site.

## Which cookies our website uses

**Essential files.** These files are needed for the basic operation of the site — for example, to display pages correctly and make forms work. They are always active and do not require separate consent, since the site cannot function properly without them.

**Analytics files.** We use Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console to understand how many people visit the site, which pages interest them, and how to improve usability. These tools are loaded **only after you give your consent**.

We do not use advertising cookies and do not show personalized ads based on your behavior on the site.

## The consent banner

On your first visit to the site, you will see a banner with two options:

- **"Accept"** — allows analytics tools to load (Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console);
- **"Essential only"** — only technically necessary elements work on the site, and analytics tools are not loaded.

Your choice is stored in your browser's localStorage under the key `td-consent`. This means your consent choice is saved locally on your device, not on our servers, and the banner will not reappear until you clear your browser data.

## How to change your choice

If you want to change a previously made choice about analytics, clear the site data (cookies and localStorage) for our website in your browser settings — the consent banner will then appear again on your next visit.

## Server logs

Separately from cookies, the hosting provider that runs our website automatically processes technical server logs (such as IP address and request time) — this is standard practice needed to run and secure any website, and it does not depend on your choice in the banner.

## Data collected by analytics

If you click "Accept", Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console may collect anonymized statistics: number of visits, pages viewed, approximate location, and device type. This data is used solely to improve the site and does not allow us to identify you personally.

For more details on how Google processes this data, see Google's own privacy policy.

## Third-party services

Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console are services provided by Google. By using them (with your consent), you also agree to Google's own terms for how it processes this data.

## Changes to this Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time — for example, when we change the set of tools we use. The current update date is shown at the top of this page.
