## Free prototype

Before starting paid work, we offer the Client a free, clickable prototype of the future website's homepage.

Terms of the free prototype:

- the prototype is delivered within 48–72 hours of receiving the request;
- one prototype is offered per company;
- the prototype creates no obligation for the Client — the Client is free to decide whether to continue working with us;
- if the prototype does not fit the Client's needs, the Client owes nothing and pays nothing.

The prototype is a demonstration of our design approach, not a finished product. Full website development begins only after the terms are agreed and payment is made.

## Payment terms

Once the Client has approved the prototype and decided to proceed, we agree on a fixed price and deadline for the project, which are recorded in an individual contract or order confirmation.

Payment is made in two steps:

1. **50% prepayment** — paid after the prototype and project terms are approved, before full-scale work begins.
2. **Remaining 50%** — paid after the work is completed and the website is launched.

For clients based in Ukraine, payments are made in Ukrainian hryvnia (UAH). For clients based abroad, payments are made in US dollars (USD). The payment method is agreed individually.

## What the Client receives

Once the project is complete and paid in full, the Client receives:

- the website's source code (or access to it);
- access to the domain, if it was registered through us;
- instructions for managing the website (if needed).

The Client becomes the full owner of the finished product and may maintain the website independently afterward, or continue working with us for support.

## Warranty

We provide a 1-year warranty from the date the website launches. The warranty covers fixes for bugs caused by our own errors during development (technical bugs, or functionality not working as agreed in the project brief).

The warranty **does not cover**:

- new design or functionality changes requested by the Client (these are treated as a new order);
- issues caused by third parties or by the Client themselves (for example, unauthorized code changes made by other developers);
- issues caused by hosting, domain, or third-party services, unless they are part of our support package.

## Hosting and domain

Hosting and an SSL certificate are included in the price for the first year after launch, for the packages where this is stated on our website. After the first year, the Client may continue hosting independently or through us, for an additional fee agreed separately.
